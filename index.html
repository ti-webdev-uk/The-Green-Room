Years of experience
— Meet Julie Holmes

A friendly welcome
and genuine expertise.

Julie is an experienced life therapist based in the heart of Saxilby, conveniently accessible from Lincoln, Gainsborough, Newark and all surrounding villages.

Having worked with people across a wide range of challenges over many years, Julie provides a warm, non-judgmental space where you're free to be yourself. She meets clients at her private practice, in your home, or at a place of your choosing.

Julie also delivers corporate counselling and coaching workshops at workplaces across Lincolnshire.
